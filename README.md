# Wholesale Distribution Analytics

A business analytics project analyzing **1M+ wholesale retail transactions** to identify revenue trends, customer behavior, product performance, seasonal patterns, geographic opportunities, and operational risks.

## Project Highlights

- Analyzed **1,067,371 raw transactions** across **41 countries**
- Cleaned and prepared **779,425 valid transaction records**
- Evaluated **£17.4M in revenue** across **36,969 orders**
- Applied **RFM customer segmentation** to identify high-value, at-risk, and dormant customers
- Performed **Pareto analysis** to measure customer revenue concentration
- Analyzed product performance, pricing, seasonality, and geographic growth
- Used **IQR-based outlier detection** to identify unusually large transactions
- Developed business recommendations for customer retention, inventory planning, product optimization, and market expansion

## Tools & Techniques

**Python · Pandas · NumPy · Matplotlib · Jupyter Notebook**

RFM Segmentation · Exploratory Data Analysis · Pareto Analysis · Product Analytics · Pricing Analysis · Geographic Analysis · Outlier Detection

## Key Finding

The **top 20% of customers generated approximately 77% of total revenue**, highlighting the importance of customer retention and account prioritization.
